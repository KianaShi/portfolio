Skyscraper07 near-foreground test

portfolio / seattle backdrop
bgtest round 2 · 2026-08-18

Follow-up to the first Skyscraper07 test. Direction changed: instead of a small, fully-visible building extending the photo's skyline, this round places one large, heavily-cropped building right outside the glass — only skyscraper07-silhouette.glb is used. Test-only, nothing here touches production.

§1

Verdict

Yes, with one fix carried forward

N3 (bottom-near, showing the roof/setback above the monitor) is the strongest of the four crops tested — real architectural silhouette, clear of both landmarks, doesn't read as a column growing out of the desk. The night-visibility problem from the first round is solved as a side effect of going bigger and closer: even with zero window lights, the building stays clearly readable at night now. Window lights work but needed two bug fixes before they were visible at all (§6) and only populate a narrow band near the roof, since most of the mesh's height is cropped away in this composition.

§4

Getting there: what didn't work first

wrong

First N1 attempt was fully invisible. At the scale/position first tried, the building's visible edge landed less than 0.1 world units past the window's own frame edge — too thin to render as more than a few dark pixels indistinguishable from the frame. Widening the visible margin to ~0.5 units (still only a sliver relative to the building's ~5-unit width at that scale) made it clearly readable.

wrong

Second N1 attempt was wildly oversized — a 1.4-unit position shift with an unchanged huge scale blocked the entire left window pane, including the Space Needle. Perspective at this close a distance (z=-1.0, ~4.85 units from camera) magnifies position changes much more than intuition suggests; every composition in §3 needed 3-4 rounds of screenshot-and-adjust to land.

wrong

N4's first placement clipped the Space Needle's observation deck. Shifting the whole composition further left (and separately, raising it enough to clear the monitor bezel below) fixed both without changing the crop character.

§5

Yaw

Tested 8° and 15° (and -8° for comparison) on N3. All three keep verticals vertical (yaw only rotates around the up axis, so it never tilts an edge into "toy model" territory) and all three show a clear two-facet corner. 15° was kept — marginally the clearest volume read of the three, still inside the requested envelope.

§6

Materials M1/M2/M3

bug, fixed

The first pass made all three materials look identical: the day/night sync was overwriting material.color with an absolute day-tint / night-tint lerp on every sync tick, which stomped each material's own base color the instant it was applied. Fixed by capturing each material's base color once and having the day/night sync apply a multiplier on top of it instead of replacing it outright — now M1/M2/M3 stay visually distinct at any blend value.

M1 (chosen)
deep grey-blue, roughness 0.85 — best facet contrast
M2
cooler, deeper than the night photo's shadow tone
M3

M1 chosen. Best light/dark separation between the roof and side facets. M3's gradient is real but this composition only ever shows the top ~8-12% of the mesh's height, leaving little room for a height gradient to read.

§7

Night window lights

Two bugs, found and fixed in sequence before any light was visible at all:

bug 1

Lights placed just inside the solid mesh, not outside it. The facade offset used surface − small margin (moving inward) instead of surface + small margin. The opaque building surface then depth-occluded its own window lights — confirmed by querying the InstancedMesh directly (visible=true, opacity=0.65, valid transforms) against a render that was pixel-identical to lights-off. Not a visibility bug, an occlusion bug.

bug 2

Lights scattered across the wrong height band. The first placement band (10-68% of the mesh's local height) is almost entirely hidden below the monitor line in N3's composition — this crop only clears the desk/monitor for roughly the top 8-12% of the mesh. Narrowing the band to 92-98.5% (and the horizontal spread to the middle 40% of each facade, away from the composition's actual visible corner) got a consistent 3-4 lights rendering per frame.

The building's own material is now a multiplier on top of each M-variant's base color (§6's fix), darkening and cooling toward night without ever forcing a fixed absolute color — so it stays a readable silhouette with facet contrast at full night, matching the brief's "not completely gone" requirement.

Background parallax F1/F2/F3

Tested reducing the shared camera-parallax coefficient (this is the same value that drives everything's screen motion, not a background-only knob) from the existing 0.15/0.08 down through three tiers, each screenshotted at its own mouse extreme:

F1 · 0.10 / 0.05
background still shifts noticeably
F2 · 0.06 / 0.03 (chosen)
background barely alive, building still clearly dominant
F3 · 0 / 0 (rejected)
kills ALL parallax, including the building's

Physical reality worth being explicit about: the building and the background react to the exact same camera translation, so their relative motion ratio is fixed by depth alone (near always moves more than far, by a constant factor) — the shared coefficient can only scale both up or down together, it cannot decouple them. F2 was chosen as the tier where the background's motion (already smaller, being far away) drops to "barely alive" while the building's motion (larger, being close) stays obviously the dominant layer. F3 (all zero) was tested and rejected: it doesn't isolate the background, it removes the depth cue entirely. tools/_bgtest/interaction-test.js's default was updated to F2; src/interaction.js (production) is untouched.

§10

Direct answers

哪个位置最像真实窗外近景？
N3 — the roof/setback geometry is what sells it; N1/N2 are technically clean but read as a flat wall, not obviously a building.
是否仍然遮挡 Space Needle 或 Rainier？
No, in any of the four final compositions, at idle camera or at either parallax extreme.
建筑边缘是否被窗框自然裁切？
Yes for N1/N2/N4 (window frame edges). N3 is cropped by the monitor/desk line instead, not the window frame.
是否出现从桌面后面长出来的感觉？
The very first N3 attempt (dead-centered on the monitor) did read that way. Offsetting it left of monitor-center fixed it — final version reads as a separate object beside the monitor, not attached to it.
两个立面是否足够表现体积？
Yes in all four — confirmed visually in every composition screenshot, clearest in N3 and N4.
哪档背景视差最能表达远近关系？
F2 (0.06/0.03). See §8 for why F3 doesn't actually achieve "background isolated from building."
夜间窗灯是否可信？
At 0.50 max intensity, yes — modest, warm, doesn't compete with the PC's RGB or the monitor glow. Coverage is thin (3-4 of 22 instances land somewhere visible) because this crop only exposes a narrow band of facade; a taller-visible composition (N4) would give the light layer more room to work with.
总 draw call、三角形和资源大小？
+2 draw calls, 1,914 triangles, 31 KB GLB (no new asset — still last round's silhouette export).
是否有明显 FPS 变化？
No measurable difference; manual 60-frame render timing showed the building's cost within normal frame-to-frame noise.
§11

Recommendation

If moving forward with this direction:

  1. Composition N3, material M1, night lights at max 0.50, background parallax F2 (0.06/0.03) — all now the defaults baked into tools/_bgtest/building-test.js and interaction-test.js.
  2. N4 is a solid fallback if N3's closeness to the monitor is judged too assertive on review.
  3. Window-light coverage is the weakest link here — only a sliver of facade is ever visible in this near-crop composition, so the light layer has little room to read as "windows," more as "a few lit dots." If lights matter more than the roof-silhouette read, N4's larger unclipped side facet would give them more room.
  4. Not attempted this round, still open: softening the building's crisp CG edge against the photo's soft focus (same open item as Round 1).
